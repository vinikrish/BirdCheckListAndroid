🐦 Bird Life List - Your Ultimate Bird Tracking Companion

Discover, track, and celebrate your bird watching adventures with Bird Life List, the comprehensive birding app designed for enthusiasts of all levels. Whether you're a casual backyard bird watcher or a serious birder traveling the world, our app makes it easy to document your sightings and build your personal life list.

✅ LOCATION-BASED BIRD LISTS
• Automatic country detection using GPS

• Filter birds by your current location
• Explore birds from around the world

✅ EASY SIGHTING TRACKING
• Simple checkbox interface to mark birds you've seen
• Add personal notes and observations
• Attach photos to your sightings
• Track when and where you spotted each bird

✅ USER-FRIENDLY DESIGN
• Clean, intuitive interface
• Fast and responsive performance

🎯 PERFECT FOR:
• Beginner bird watchers learning to identify species

1. Open the app and let it detect your location
