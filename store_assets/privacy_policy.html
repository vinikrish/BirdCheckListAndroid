Privacy Policy for Bird Checklist

Last updated: September 21, 2025

1. Introduction

Bird Checklist ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide

  • Bird sighting records and observations
  • Location data for bird sightings (when you choose to share it)
  • User preferences and app settings
  • Account information (if you create an account)

2.2 Automatically Collected Information

  • Device information (model, operating system version)
  • App usage statistics and crash reports
  • Location data (only when you grant permission and use location features)

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:

  • Provide and maintain the app's functionality
  • Save and sync your bird sighting records
  • Improve app performance and user experience
  • Provide location-based features (when enabled)
  • Send important app updates and notifications

4. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored locally on your device and may be backed up to secure cloud services (Google Drive, Firebase) if you enable sync features. We implement appropriate security measures to protect your personal information.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal information with third parties, except:

  • When required by law
  • To protect our rights and safety
  • With your explicit consent
  • Anonymous, aggregated data for research purposes (no personal identification)

6. Location Data

Location data is only collected when you:

  • Grant location permissions
  • Use location-based features
  • Manually add location to bird sightings

You can disable location features at any time in your device settings.

7. Children's Privacy

Our app is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without parental consent. If you believe we have collected such information, please contact us immediately.

8. Third-Party Services

Our app may use third-party services such as:

  • Google Play Services
  • Firebase (Google)
  • Crash reporting services

These services have their own privacy policies governing their use of information.

9. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Delete your data
  • Export your data
  • Withdraw consent for data processing

10. Data Retention

We retain your data as long as you use the app. You can delete your data at any time through the app settings or by uninstalling the app.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy in the app and updating the "Last updated" date.
